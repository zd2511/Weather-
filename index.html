Settings & data
Primary region
Vanderbijlpark / Vaal Triangle

The app is optimised for local weather around Vanderbijlpark, Nanescol AH and surrounding areas.

Data architecture

Forecast: Open-Meteo multi-model weather data. Radar: RainViewer. Satellite: EUMETSAT Earth View. Base map: OpenStreetMap. The app caches the last successful forecast locally so the last data remains visible when temporarily offline.

Refresh
Accuracy note

No forecast can guarantee conditions at a particular house, especially during fast-forming Gauteng thunderstorms. This app exposes model agreement and observational radar/satellite context instead of presenting uncertain forecasts as certain.
